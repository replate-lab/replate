Design point

Airflow is the thesis. The blue path is what actually removes water. Heat alone leaves hot wet waste, vapour has to be carried out. More air dries faster and costs more energy per kg. The optimum is measured, not calculated.
Not a simulation. Geometry from the sizing calculations, not from a solver. No CFD, no thermal FEA. Call it a design model.
